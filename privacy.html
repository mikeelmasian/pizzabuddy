Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy covers the Pizza Buddy mobile apps, the web calculator and this website (pizzabuddy.io), all from Corvega Studios ("we", "us").

What we collect

There are no accounts, no sign-up and no ads. We don't collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. Your style, size and count choices stay on your device while you use the app.

Analytics

This website and the web calculator use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and features are used. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your IP address, browser and device information, and the pages you view. We use this data only in aggregate to improve Pizza Buddy; we don't try to identify you and we don't use it for advertising. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can opt out with Google's browser add-on or by blocking cookies or scripts in your browser.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses. We don't have access to those logs.

Shopping links

Pizza Buddy shows product suggestions with links to Amazon. When you tap one, Amazon's website or app opens, and Amazon's own privacy policy applies from there. We don't see what you do there. If we join the Amazon Associates program, we may earn a commission on qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you, and the app and site will say so.

Third parties

The app stores you download from (Apple App Store, Google Play) may collect their own data under their own policies, which we don't control.

Children

Pizza Buddy isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. If we ever add data collection, we'll update this page before the change ships.
